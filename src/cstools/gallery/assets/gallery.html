Comparison theme dumps

Use the packaged cstools/gallery/assets/dump.lua with headless Neovim: CS=theme PRE='vim.opt.rtp:prepend("/path/to/theme-runtime")' OUT=/tmp/theme.json nvim --headless -u NONE -l /path/to/dump.lua. Use disposable XDG_CONFIG_HOME, XDG_CACHE_HOME, XDG_DATA_HOME and XDG_STATE_HOME directories. Then rebuild with cstools gallery REPO -o OUTDIR --extra-dumps /path/to/dumps. Full instructions are in the packaged assets/README.md.
